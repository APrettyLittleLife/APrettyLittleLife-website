Website use

This site does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website.

Inquiries

The inquiry forms are not connected to a submission service yet and do not transmit your details. Once business email is configured, preparing an inquiry opens your email application for you to review and send. A hosted form service, if enabled, will receive the information you submit; its provider and privacy terms must be identified here before launch.

Payments and external links

Purchases take place on Square, which processes payment and shipping information under its own terms and privacy policy. Social platforms and other external sites have their own privacy practices.
